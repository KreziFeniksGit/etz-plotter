# Open belts clamped on the carriage

**Egor, 12 Oct.** GT2 belts are open lengths; both ends are clamped on the carriage (Y clamp 30 mm / 3 screws,
X 42 mm / 3 screws); tension at idler sliders pushed by an M3 screw; motors fixed.

Why: no closed loop to buy in exact length; tension is adjustable; the clamp is a printed part we control.
Feeds the [[Backlash budget]] (0.11 mm Y, 0.07 mm X). Related: [[GT2 belts and pulleys]], [[FEA]].
