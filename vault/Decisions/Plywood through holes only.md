# Plywood through holes only

**Egor, 23 Oct (v2.15):** the plywood shop drills through holes only - no recesses, counterbores, countersinks.
So feet sit flat under the plywood and Y beam bolts are M5x22 with heads underneath.
Also 22 Oct: no wood screws - every part is bolted through with a washer and nut; `near_table` check: only feet within 2 mm of the table.
DXF for the shop: `tools/plywood_dxf.py`. Related: [[Printed PETG feet]], [[Fasteners model]], [[Mechanics]].
