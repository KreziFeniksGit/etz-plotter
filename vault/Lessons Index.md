# Lessons Index

Mistakes and surprises, each turned into a rule. Read the ones for your area before starting.

**Process / Claude**
- [[Commit and push right away - sessions die]]
- [[A failed command in a chain silently skips the rest]]
- [[Use the Edit tool, not shell tricks, on Windows]]
- [[Rules that cannot bend belong in hooks]]
- [[Say plainly what was only compiled or simulated]]
- [[Egor changes parts often - confirm before investing]]
- [[Never guess a bought-part dimension - mark VERIFY]]
- [[Check coordinates against the official document]]

**CAD**
- [[FreeCAD 1.1 failed fillet damages the shape]]
- [[Part.export without the GUI writes an empty STEP]]
- [[Sweeps must sample where things actually pass]]
- [[Check that a human can assemble it]]
- [[Vendor models can be the wrong variant]]
- [[Non-ASCII paths break external tools]]
- [[Check which way a change moves the reach]]

**Electronics / firmware**
- [[Check the wiring before blaming the firmware]]
- [[Fix crossed cables in pins.h, not with a soldering iron]]
- [[Verify ADC readings with a multimeter]]
- [[GPIO38 and GPIO48 are inputs only]]
- [[Battery left connected drains through the logic regulator]]
- [[Route PCB traces only at 0, 45 and 90 degrees]]
- [[Firmware defaults must meet the rules]]

**Laptop software**
- [[The twin finds real bugs before the hardware exists]]
- [[Do not react to your own abort messages]]

**Theory round** (Egor's error types, from `theory/progress.md`)
- [[Write units on every line]]
- [[Two limits - take the smaller, never subtract]]
- [[Inertial force needs the mass]]
- [[Lever arm is the perpendicular distance]]
- [[Count operations before writing code]]
- [[Read indexing and fenceposts twice]]
- [[A gearbox multiplies torque by its ratio]]
