# Never guess a bought-part dimension - mark VERIFY

**What happened:** Several sizes came from third-party models (MG996R tabs, motor shafts, bracket holes) and could be wrong.

**Rule:** Unknown sizes get `VERIFY` in the script and a line in Open Items; ask Egor to measure with calipers.

Related: [[Design rules (DFM)]], [[Open Items]], [[Vendor models can be the wrong variant]]
