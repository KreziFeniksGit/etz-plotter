# Battery left connected drains through the logic regulator

**What happened:** The logic regulator is not switched by the E-stop and the boards have no power switch: a parked plotter slowly drains the LiPo.

**Rule:** Unplug the XT60 after practice; add an inline switch if possible. Deep-discharged LiPo = dead cell.
