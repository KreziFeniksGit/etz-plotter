# Egor changes parts often - confirm before investing

**What happened:** Servo went DS3218 -> MG996R -> RDS3235 -> MG996R in a week; wrist NEMA11 -> NEMA17 pancake; plywood 12 -> 6 mm.

**Rule:** Before a big model change for a new part, confirm the choice and keep the old version easy to restore (git). Do not polish what may be dropped.
