# Vendor models can be the wrong variant

**What happened:** The 684 bearing model in `vendor/` is the open 2.5 mm version; the seats need the shielded 684ZZ (4 mm).

**Rule:** Check a vendor STEP against the exact part number being bought; write the variant in `components.csv`.
