# Say plainly what was only compiled or simulated

**What happened:** Firmware v5 builds clean and matches the twin but never ran on a board; the twin's capture radius and servo timing are guesses.

**Rule:** Every report says which level of proof it has: modelled, compiled, twin-tested, bench-tested, built-and-tested.
