# Verify ADC readings with a multimeter

**What happened:** The board shows 11.8 V for a 4S battery (should be 14.8-16.8). Divider, ADC calibration or a flat battery - unknown. With the battery guard it would refuse every move.

**Rule:** Before trusting a measured voltage, measure the same point with a multimeter and calibrate the factor.
