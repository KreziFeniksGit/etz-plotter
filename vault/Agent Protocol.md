How Claude works here. Full text: `CLAUDE.md` and `.claude/rules/*.md` (cad, firmware, laptop, pcb load by path).

- **Doubt, compare, research, find risks** for features and design choices (not for small fixes) - Egor's request 27 Oct.
- **Check your own work** with the area's checks; report failures honestly: [[Say plainly what was only compiled or simulated]].
- **Commit and push right away**: [[Commit and push right away - sessions die]]. A stop hook enforces it: [[Rules that cannot bend belong in hooks]].
- **Windows shell:** [[Use the Edit tool, not shell tricks, on Windows]], [[A failed command in a chain silently skips the rest]].
- **Never guess** a bought-part size: [[Never guess a bought-part dimension - mark VERIFY]].
- **Separate sessions** for separate jobs (firmware v5, theory prep); verify their result before telling Egor it is done.
- **Learn:** a correction made twice becomes a rule (CLAUDE.md or a rule file) and a [[Lessons Index]] entry.
