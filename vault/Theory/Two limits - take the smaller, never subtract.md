# Two limits - take the smaller, never subtract

**Seen:** Set 02 / 5 and set 03 / 4: motor torque limit and friction limit mixed into one formula or subtracted.

**Rule:** Compute each limit separately (motor, traction, tipping); the real maximum is the smallest of them.
