# Dynamics and traction

F = ma; in an accelerating frame add the inertial force −m·a at the centre of mass (arm h·cos θ). Traction limit F <= μN;
wheel force from motor torque: F = M·i·η / r (r = radius!). Balancing/tilting: a = g·tan θ.
