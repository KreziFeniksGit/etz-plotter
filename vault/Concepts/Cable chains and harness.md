# Cable chains and harness

Real X and Y cable chains rebuilt per pose (`frame/chains.py`), harness tubes per group (`frame/cables.py`); the wrist harness
is rebuilt per pose. Magnet leads are a loose coil, not modelled. Related: [[Zip-tie features]], [[Interference sweep]], [[Pin map]].
