# Parallelogram lift

The [[MG996R servo]] turns an 80 mm parallelogram so the magnet face stays level while moving 5..40 mm (a stack of 5 = 25 mm).
Arm angle th = asin((h − 22.5)/80). Pivots on 684ZZ bearings ([[Vendor models can be the wrong variant]]).
Theory link: [[Statics and torque]] (servo holding torque), kinematics of linkages. Related: [[Mechanics]].
