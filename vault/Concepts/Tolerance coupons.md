# Tolerance coupons

11 small test prints (`tolerance/`) that measure what fits on Egor's printer: bolt holes, press fits, bearings, motor bosses, inserts...
Result so far: A2 -> M4/M5 holes fit at +0 (TOL_BOLT_D 0.0, 21 Oct). The rest not printed yet ([[Open Items]]).
