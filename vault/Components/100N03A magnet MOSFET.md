# 100N03A magnet MOSFET

UMW 100N03A N-MOSFET (TO-252), logic level (Vgs(th) 1-2.5 V, 6.4 mΩ at 4.5 V) switches the magnet from a 3.3 V pin;
SS34 flyback diode; gate 100 R series + 100 k to GND. **Egor, 23 Oct.**
One low-side switch = can only turn the current off, not reverse it: [[Magnet release]].
