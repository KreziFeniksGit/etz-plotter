# P20-15 electromagnet

Holding electromagnet d20 x 15, 5 V, ~25 N; the checker is 7 g with an M8 washer inside.
Switched by the [[100N03A magnet MOSFET]]; firmware: full power 150 ms to pull across the gap, then 40 % hold; off after 30 s.
Release problem: [[Magnet release]]. Screwed on an M3 set-screw stud in a Ruthex insert ([[Check that a human can assemble it]]).
The [[Stop button]]'s second contact cuts its 5 V. Related: [[Mechanics]], [[Electronics]].
