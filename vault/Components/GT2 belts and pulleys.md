# GT2 belts and pulleys

GT2 6 mm belt (Gates if possible: less stretch), 20T pulleys with two grub screws (one on the shaft flat, Loctite 243 - no slip = no backlash), toothless idlers.
20T = 40 mm per turn; at 16 microsteps -> 80 steps/mm; 100 mm/s = 150 rpm = 8000 steps/s ([[ETZ speed minimum]]).
