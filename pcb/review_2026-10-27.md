# PCB review, 27 Oct 2026 - split boards (drivers, power, controller)

Tool: kicad-happy `kicad` skill, `analyze_pcb.py --full` on each `.kicad_pcb` (analyzer schema 1.4), plus manual checks
of the analyzer data (trace current, connector polarity, divider / MOSFET circuits). Boards: single-sided, bottom copper,
home toner transfer, hand-routed. Drivers + controller are built and working; power board built after the XT30 fix.

## Verdict
**No board-killing errors found in the layouts.** All three are fully routed (0 unrouted nets) and no net is split into
separate copper islands (union-find over pads, tracks and the GND pour). Two real risks are outside the copper: the
battery voltage reading and the reversed XT30 on the drivers board (see below).

## Checked and good
- Motor coil tracks 1.0 mm (~2.3 A) for 0.9 A RMS (1.27 A peak); VDRIVE on the drivers board 2.0 mm (~4 A).
- Logic / UART / STEP / DIR 0.6 mm; GND pour on B.Cu on every board (fill 67-80 %), one island each.
- Magnet switch: 100 R gate resistor + 100 k pull-down (magnet stays off while the ESP32 boots), SS34 flyback across
  the magnet in the right direction (cathode on +5 V side). 100N03A switching ~0.5 A: a few mW, no heatsinking needed.
- Controller D1 SS34: cathode on 5V_ESP, anode on +5V: the power board feeds the ESP32, USB cannot back-feed the power board.
- Connector A / B / C inputs: 10 k / 20 k dividers (5 V -> 3.33 V); C TX 1 k; I2C 4.7 k pull-ups to 3V3; 0.5 A PTC on the C supply.
- Bulk caps: 100 uF 25 V per driver (4S max 16.8 V = 67 % of rating), 470 uF 16 V on the servo rail, 100 uF 10 V on +5 V.
- Battery fuse: 5 A blade fuse in the battery lead (wiring.md), not on the boards.

## Not performed / limits
- **Schematic analysis, cross-analysis, EMC, thermal**: the split boards have no schematic (nets were written straight into
  the PCB by `build_*.py`); the old `etz_control.kicad_sch` is the earlier single-board design. These need a schematic.
- **SPICE**: no simulator installed (ngspice / LTspice / Xyce).
- **Datasheet verification**: no `datasheets/` folder and no part numbers in the footprints. Pin-level checks are
  consistency only (board agrees with itself), not verified against datasheets. Highest-value datasheet checks if wanted:
  TMC2209 module pin order (BTT V1.3), 100N03A pinout (G-D-S on TO-252), Mini560 Pro pin order, ESP32-S3-DevKitC-1 pinout.
  The boards work on the bench, which already confirms the driver and ESP32 pinouts.
- **Gerbers, lifecycle audit**: no Gerber files (toner transfer from SVG); no part numbers for a lifecycle lookup.
